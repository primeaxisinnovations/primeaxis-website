<p>The Nigerian Navy has confirmed the interception of a crude oil carrier off the Bonny Channel during a joint patrol operation conducted with assets deployed under the Deep Blue Project. The vessel was detained on suspicion of irregularities in its cargo documentation.</p><p>A statement from Naval Headquarters said the vessel was escorted to the Naval Dockyard in Port Harcourt, where its crew is assisting an ongoing investigation. No injuries were reported and the vessel's cargo remains secured on board pending the outcome of the documentation review.</p><p>The interception is the fourth of its kind reported in the Eastern Naval Command's area of responsibility this quarter, and comes as regional enforcement agencies intensify scrutiny of cargo provenance in Nigeria's crude export corridors.</p>
