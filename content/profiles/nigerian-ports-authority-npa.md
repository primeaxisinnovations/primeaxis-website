  The Nigerian Ports Authority (NPA) is responsible for the administration,
  management, and development of Nigeria’s seaports and port infrastructure. The
  Authority facilitates maritime trade by supporting efficient vessel
  operations, cargo handling, port services, and the development of modern port
  infrastructure. NPA is central to Nigeria’s position as a regional maritime
  and logistics hub and plays a significant role in strengthening the country’s
  trade and Blue Economy.</p>
