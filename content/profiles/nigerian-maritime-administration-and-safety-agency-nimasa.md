  The Nigerian Maritime Administration and Safety Agency (NIMASA) is Nigeria’s
  apex maritime regulatory and administration agency, responsible for promoting
  maritime safety, security, shipping development, seafarer standards, and
  sustainable maritime practices. The agency plays a central role in regulating
  Nigeria’s shipping industry, strengthening indigenous maritime capacity, and
  supporting the growth of the country’s Blue Economy.</p>
