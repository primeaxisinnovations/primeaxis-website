<p class="MsoNormal">The Nigerian Maritime Administration and Safety Agency
(NIMASA) have conducted a coordinated enforcement operation across key
operational areas in Lagos, deploying assets of the Integrated National
Security and Waterways Protection Infrastructure, known as the Deep Blue
structure, alongside relevant maritime security units.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">Codenamed Operation Blue Green, the operation combined
intelligence gathering, analysis and target development with coordinated
enforcement at sea.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">A new specialised intelligence capability within NIMASA’s
Deep Blue and Command, Control, Communications, Computers and Intelligence
System (C4i) was deployed during the operation to improve the identification,
assessment and prioritisation of vessels of interest and provide real time
intelligence support to enforcement teams.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">The development gives the enforcement exercise a stronger
intelligence dimension, connecting the identification of vessels of interest
more directly with operational action at sea.<o:p></o:p></p>

<h2>From Intelligence to Operational Priority<o:p></o:p></h2>

<p class="MsoNormal">During Operation Blue Green, intelligence gathering,
analysis and target development supported the coordinated deployment of
enforcement teams, while real time intelligence provided operational support
during the exercise.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">This creates a more focused link between information and
enforcement action.<br>
Intelligence Meets Compliance. Several vessels were boarded and inspected
during the operation for document verification and compliance screening.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">The inspections covered statutory certification, cargo and
voyage documentation, surcharge obligations and cabotage compliance
requirements.<br>
The physical inspections provided the enforcement component of the intelligence
led approach, allowing identified vessels to be assessed against relevant
regulatory requirements.<br>
The significance lies in the connection between target development and
compliance verification.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><b><i>A Model NIMASA Plans to Repeat</i></b><o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">The Director General of NIMASA, Dr. Dayo Mobereola, said the
Agency will formalise Blue Green as a recurring intelligence led enforcement
model.<br>
He stated that similar operations will progressively expand to other maritime
regions, including Port Harcourt, Calabar and Warri, in line with operational
readiness and intelligence priorities.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><o:p>&nbsp;</o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">This moves Blue Green beyond a single Lagos operation.<br>
The proposed recurring model would bring intelligence gathering, analysis,
target development and coordinated enforcement into a continuing operational
framework.<br>
Its effectiveness will depend on how consistently the intelligence capability
can support enforcement priorities and how effectively operational findings
contribute to subsequent compliance activity.<o:p></o:p></p>

<h2>Coordinated Enforcement<o:p></o:p></h2>

<p class="MsoNormal">Operation Blue Green involved multiple enforcement platforms
and cooperation from the armed forces.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><b><i>Dr. Mobereola</i></b> described the deployment as a
deterrent to anyone with criminal intentions and expressed satisfaction with
the level of cooperation from the armed forces.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">That cooperation provides the operational capacity required
to translate intelligence priorities into action at sea.<o:p></o:p></p>

<h2>Compliance and Revenue<o:p></o:p></h2>

<p class="MsoNormal">The operation also carries a revenue dimension.<br>
The inspections covered surcharge obligations alongside statutory
certification, cargo and voyage documentation and cabotage compliance
requirements.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><b><i>NIMASA</i></b> stated that Operation Blue Green
provides a foundation for more structured and sustained intelligence led
enforcement aimed at strengthening maritime compliance and supporting improved
revenue generation.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">This connects the intelligence capability to a broader
regulatory objective. <br>
Better target prioritisation can support more focused compliance checks, while
enforcement findings can help establish whether relevant obligations are being
met.<o:p></o:p></p>

<h2>PrimeAxis Insight<o:p></o:p></h2>

<p class="MsoNormal">Operation Blue Green is significant because it places
intelligence closer to the point where maritime enforcement decisions are made.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">The specialised capability introduced within NIMASA’s C4i
structure gives the Agency an additional mechanism for identifying, assessing
and prioritising vessels of interest while supporting enforcement teams in real
time.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">The larger opportunity lies in what happens when that
capability becomes recurring.<br>
A single operation can demonstrate that intelligence and enforcement can work
together.<br>
A sustained model can show whether that capability improves operational
priorities, sharpens enforcement focus and strengthens compliance outcomes. <b><i>That
is the real test of Blue Green.</i></b><o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><o:p>&nbsp;</o:p></p>

<blockquote><p class="MsoNormal"><b><i>NIMASA</i></b> is not simply deploying maritime
assets.<o:p></o:p></p></blockquote>

<p class="MsoNormal">It is developing a process in which intelligence can help
determine where those assets are directed and what they are deployed to
address.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">The proposed expansion to Port Harcourt, Calabar and Warri
will provide further scope for that model to develop.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">For Nigeria’s maritime regulatory environment, the value
will ultimately be measured by stronger compliance, more effective enforcement
and improved visibility over activities requiring regulatory attention.<o:p></o:p></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><b><i>Blue Green therefore represents more than an
enforcement operation in Lagos.<o:p></o:p></i></b></p>

<p class="MsoNormal">It points towards a maritime compliance model in which
intelligence informs priority, operational capability delivers enforcement and
enforcement strengthens the compliance picture. <br>
The next measure of its significance will be whether that model can become a
consistent part of maritime compliance across Nigeria’s operational waters.</p>
