<h2><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="background-color: transparent; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: Calibri, sans-serif; font-size: 11pt; font-weight: 400; white-space: pre-wrap;">Nigeria’s port economy is entering a more consequential phase of regulation.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">The Nigeria Ports Economic Regulatory Agency, NPERA, is moving from regulatory authority to enforcement, with Director General and Chief Executive Officer, Dr. Pius Akutah, declaring that the NPERA Act 2026 will be fully enforced, including provisions for fines and criminal prosecution.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">The declaration signals a significant change in the regulatory environment around Nigeria’s ports.
</span><span style="background-color: transparent; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: Calibri, sans-serif; font-size: 11pt; font-weight: 400; white-space: pre-wrap;">Under the former Nigerian Shippers’ Council framework, Akutah said regulatory provisions were largely persuasive and lacked sufficient enforcement power to create meaningful deterrence. The new law changes that equation by giving NPERA stronger sanctions against infractions relating to freight, tariffs, charges and service delivery.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">The significance is not simply that NPERA can now punish violations.
</span><span style="background-color: transparent; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: Calibri, sans-serif; font-size: 11pt; font-weight: 400; white-space: pre-wrap;">It is that noncompliance now carries consequences that can materially affect businesses operating within Nigeria’s port economy.
</span><span style="background-color: transparent; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: Calibri, sans-serif; font-size: 11pt; font-weight: 400; white-space: pre-wrap;">For an industry where the cost, speed and predictability of moving cargo directly affect trade competitiveness, that changes the commercial calculation.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">The question now is what full enforcement will actually change across Nigeria’s ports.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;"><br></span></p></h2><h3><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">WHEN COMPLIANCE HAS A COST</span></h3><h2><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">Akutah has disclosed that the new framework provides for a minimum fine of ₦500,000 for an individual first offender and up to ₦20 million for a corporation, with the possibility of increased penalties for continuing violations. Criminal prosecution is also available in applicable cases.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">The numbers matter, but the larger issue is deterrence.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">Regulation becomes effective when the cost of violating a rule begins to outweigh the benefit of ignoring it.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">For businesses operating across Nigeria’s port economy, that calculation could now change.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">Terminal operators, shipping companies, logistics providers and other regulated entities will have to factor regulatory compliance more directly into their operations, particularly where violations concern charges, service standards or other commercial practices within NPERA’s mandate.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">The objective, however, should not be simply to collect fines. It should be to make compliance the more rational commercial choice.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">That distinction matters because the success of an enforcement regime should ultimately be measured by fewer violations, better service and greater certainty, not by the volume of penalties imposed.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="margin: 0pt 0px 10pt; color: rgb(13, 31, 40); font-family: Montserrat, &quot;Montserrat Fallback&quot;, system-ui, sans-serif; font-size: 15px; font-weight: 400; line-height: 1.38;"><b style="font-weight:normal;" id="docs-internal-guid-1e0e569c-7fff-d634-2bce-5fbc924be4e8"><br></b></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">And that brings the focus beyond the sanction itself to the machinery required to make enforcement credible.</span></p></h2><h2 dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">FROM REGULATION TO INTELLIGENCE</span></h2><h2><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">A Agency’s renewed engagement with the Maritime Anti Corruption Network, MACN, is therefore strategically relevant. The partnership is expected to strengthen access to data, global experience and training, while supporting incident reporting, transparent escalation and more measurable outcomes through a more value of such cooperation extends beyond individual complaints.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span reports, regulatory data and industry intelligence can help reveal where violations occur, how frequently they occur and whether apparently isolated incidents point to wider patterns.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">That creates the possibility of a more intelligent enforcement model.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">Instead of waiting for problems to surface through individual disputes, NPERA can use information to identify risks, target intervention and measure whether corrective action is working.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span a port system as complex as Nigeria’s, that distinction matters.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span regulation increasingly depends not only on the rules themselves, but on the quality of the information available to the regulator.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">NPERA also plans to establish a dedicated Enforcement Department supported by trained security personnel posted directly to the Agency and under its control. The stated objective is to strengthen compliance and protect the enforcement process from compromise.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">That is an important institutional development because the effectiveness of the Act will depend not only on what the law permits, but on whether NPERA has the capacity to investigate suspected violations, establish facts and follow through on enforcement decisions.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">But greater enforcement power also raises the standard expected of the regulator itself.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">Rules must be clear. Investigations must be credible. Decisions must be consistent.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">Enforcement must be sufficiently transparent to command confidence among operators and port users.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">The strength of a regulator is not measured only by its ability to impose sanctions. It is also measured by the predictability of the system it creates.</span></p></h2><h2 dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">THE REAL TEST OF ENFORCEMENT</span></h2><h2><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">Freight, tariffs, charges and service delivery sit close to the centre of NPERA’s enforcement agenda. These are some of the most commercially sensitive areas of Nigeria’s port economy.</span></p><p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.3800000000000001;margin-top:0pt;margin-bottom:10pt;"><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Calibri,sans-serif;color:#000000;background-color:transparent;font-weight:400;font-style:normal;font-variant:normal;text-decoration:none;vertical-align:baseline;white-space:pre;white-space:pre-wrap;">For businesses moving cargo through Nigerian ports, the issue is not simply what a service costs.
