<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 9pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">The development of
Lagos State’s riverine communities depends not only on investment in
infrastructure and public services but also on how effectively government
responds to the needs of the people who live there. A youth dialogue held at
Badore Ferry Terminal, Ajah, has brought this challenge into focus, placing
grassroots participation at the centre of discussions on inclusive development.<o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 0.0001pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">&nbsp;</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 9pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">The Blue Lagos
Sustainability Initiative, in collaboration with the New Generation
Directorate, convened 250 young people representing 125 riverine and coastal
communities across Lagos State to discuss local challenges, development
priorities and opportunities for stronger engagement with government.<o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 0.0001pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">&nbsp;</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 9pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">The dialogue provided
a platform for participants to identify gaps in essential services and present
recommendations on issues affecting their communities. These included
education, healthcare, employment, transportation, infrastructure,
environmental sustainability, youth empowerment and access to government
programmes.<o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 0.0001pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">&nbsp;</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 9pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">For riverine
settlements, geographical barriers can complicate access to essential services
and economic opportunities. Where waterways are central to daily movement, the
availability, affordability and safety of transportation can influence access
to schools, healthcare facilities, markets and workplaces.<o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 0.0001pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">&nbsp;</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 9pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">The engagement
therefore raises an important question about how government can better
incorporate the realities of these communities into development planning.<o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin: 15pt 0in 7.5pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><b><span style="font-size:16.0pt;font-family:&quot;Georgia&quot;,serif;

color:#151C2E">FROM COMMUNITY CONCERNS TO GOVERNMENT ACTION<o:p></o:p></span></b></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 9pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">Speaking at the event,
Mr Oluwadamilola Emmanuel, Special Adviser to the Governor of Lagos State on
Blue Economy and host of the dialogue, emphasised the importance of engaging
young people in governance and the development of coastal and riverine communities.<o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 0.0001pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">&nbsp;</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 9pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">He stressed the need
for effective communication between government and grassroots communities,
noting that meaningful development requires listening to residents,
understanding their needs and incorporating their perspectives into planning
and implementation.<o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 0.0001pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><!--[if gte vml 1]><v:shapetype

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 0.0001pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">&nbsp;</span></p>

significance for Lagos State’s blue economy ambitions. The state’s waterways
and coastal settlements support economic activities and livelihoods linked to
transportation, trade, tourism and other maritime related services. Yet the potential
of this ecosystem cannot be assessed solely through commercial investment and
economic output. The welfare of communities that depend on these waterways must
also form part of the development equation.<o:p></o:p></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin-bottom: 0.0001pt; line-height: normal; background-image: initial; background-position: initial; background-size: initial; background-repeat: initial; background-attachment: initial; background-origin: initial; background-clip: initial; border-width: medium; border-style: none; padding: 0in;"><span style="font-size: 11.5pt; font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">&nbsp;</span></p>

sustainability, access to essential services and economic opportunity are
interconnected. The condition of waterways affects livelihoods, while
inadequate transportation and infrastructure can limit access to education,
healthcare and employment. Addressing these challenges requires development
interventions that reflect the circumstances of individual communities and
coordination among the relevant government institutions.<o:p></o:p></span></p>
